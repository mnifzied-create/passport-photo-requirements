# Where glasses are not allowed in passport and visa photos

23 of the 112 passport, visa and ID documents whose official rules mention glasses do not allow them at all. The list by country, each from the issuing authority's own page.

Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id). Data: [CC BY 4.0](../LICENSE).

Of the 207 passport, visa and ID documents in WinPhoto ID's table, the official rules of 112 say something about glasses. For 23 of them glasses are not allowed at all; for 8 they are allowed only if you have to wear them; for 72 they are allowed with no tint and no glare; and 9 allow them with the eyes clearly visible. The rules were read between 4 November 2026 and 19 November 2026. Only the issuing authority decides whether a photo is accepted.

## Where they are allowed

The other 81 documents allow glasses: 72 only with clear lenses and no glare on them, 9 with the eyes clearly visible. Every one is in the glasses column of [the full table](https://winphoto.io/id/photo-sizes). If glare is what fails your photo, [this page](https://winphoto.io/id/fix/glasses-glare) shows how to take it away.

## Where the figures come from

Each row is one document in WinPhoto ID's table of 207 passport, visa and ID photo rules, read on the issuing authority's own page; the date in each row is the day that page was read. The whole table is [on one page](https://winphoto.io/id/photo-sizes), and the same data is open on GitHub under CC BY 4.0: [passport-photo-requirements](https://github.com/mnifzied-create/passport-photo-requirements). Only the issuing authority decides whether a photo is accepted. Each document's page links its official source, and most have a [free photo checker](https://winphoto.io/id) that measures your photo against those rules, in your browser.

## Questions

### Can I wear glasses in a passport photo?

It depends on the document. Of the 112 documents whose rules mention glasses, 23 do not allow them at all and 8 allow them only if you have to wear them; the rest allow them with clear lenses and no glare. The tables above list each one.
