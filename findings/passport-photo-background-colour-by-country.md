# Passport photo background colour by country

Of 179 passport, visa and ID photo rules, 85 ask for white alone and 7 say the background must not be white. Every document's background rule, by country, from the official page.

Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id). Data: [CC BY 4.0](../LICENSE).

White is the most common background for an official photo, but not the only one. Of the 207 documents in WinPhoto ID's table, 179 have a background rule on record: 85 ask for white alone, 94 accept or ask for another light colour, and 7 say the background must not be white. The rules were read between 4 November 2026 and 20 November 2026. Only the issuing authority decides whether a photo is accepted.

The background column gives the rule in the official page's terms, shortened; each document's page quotes it in full. A photo with the wrong background: [what to do](https://winphoto.io/id/fix/background-not-white).

## Where the figures come from

Each row is one document in WinPhoto ID's table of 207 passport, visa and ID photo rules, read on the issuing authority's own page; the date in each row is the day that page was read. The whole table is [on one page](https://winphoto.io/id/photo-sizes), and the same data is open on GitHub under CC BY 4.0: [passport-photo-requirements](https://github.com/mnifzied-create/passport-photo-requirements). Only the issuing authority decides whether a photo is accepted. Each document's page links its official source, and most have a [free photo checker](https://winphoto.io/id) that measures your photo against those rules, in your browser.

## Questions

### What colour background does a passport photo need?

Of the 179 documents with a background rule in the table, 85 ask for white alone; the others accept or ask for off-white, light grey, cream or another plain light colour, and 7 say the background must not be white. The tables above give each document's rule.

### Which passport photos must not have a white background?

French driving licence photo (France); French passport photo (France); French residence permit photo (France); OCI photo (India); Kiwi Access Card photo (New Zealand); New Zealand visa photo (New Zealand); NZeTA photo (New Zealand).
