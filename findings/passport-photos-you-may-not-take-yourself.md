# Passport photos you may not take yourself

11 passport, visa and ID documents ask for a photo taken by a photographer or studio, and 12 more say someone else must take it: no selfie. The list by country, from the official rules.

Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id). Data: [CC BY 4.0](../LICENSE).

Most official photo rules say what the photo must look like, not who takes it. Of the 207 documents in WinPhoto ID's table, 11 ask for a photo taken by a commercial photographer or studio, and 12 more say someone else must take it, which rules out a selfie. The rules were read between 4 November 2026 and 18 November 2026. Only the issuing authority decides whether a photo is accepted.

## Everywhere else

The other 184 documents in the table set no such limit: their rules say nothing about who takes the photo, allow anyone to, or have the authority take it when you apply; each document's page says which.

## Where the figures come from

Each row is one document in WinPhoto ID's table of 207 passport, visa and ID photo rules, read on the issuing authority's own page; the date in each row is the day that page was read. The whole table is [on one page](https://winphoto.io/id/photo-sizes), and the same data is open on GitHub under CC BY 4.0: [passport-photo-requirements](https://github.com/mnifzied-create/passport-photo-requirements). Only the issuing authority decides whether a photo is accepted. Each document's page links its official source, and most have a [free photo checker](https://winphoto.io/id) that measures your photo against those rules, in your browser.
