# Italy: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 3 Italy documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-19. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Italian driving licence (patente di guida), printed photo for the Motorizzazione application or the renewal medical

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high (the Ministry allows 32-35 mm wide x 40-45 mm high)
- **Background:** uniform and evenly lit, preferably light grey, cream or light blue, or white (MIT circular 23176/8.3); white for a first licence application (MIT circular 28819 of 2019, 'su fondo bianco'); no shadows on the face or background
- **Glasses:** allowed, eyes clearly visible
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://www.ilportaledellautomobilista.it/documents/56611/25358733/Fotografie+sulla+patente.pdf/37e6a2c5-4dd0-4809-a3f9-e1f95d6dd8c6>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/it_driving_licence>

## Italian electronic identity card (CIE, Carta d'Identità Elettronica), printed photo 35 x 45 mm for the Comune

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high (45 mm is the minimum height)
- **Background:** uniform and evenly lit, preferably light blue, beige, light brown, light grey or white, to contrast with the face; no shadows on the background
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.cartaidentita.interno.gov.it/richiedi/modalita-di-acquisizione-delle-foto/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/it_cie>

## Italian electronic passport, printed photo (Questura, Commissariato or Italian consulate)

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** white, evenly lit; nothing and no one else in the background
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/il-rilascio>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/it_passport>
