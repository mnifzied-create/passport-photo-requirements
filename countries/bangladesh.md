# Bangladesh: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 2 Bangladesh documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-16. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Bangladesh e-passport (photo captured at biometric enrolment)

- **Photo size:** 3R (under 6 only; no further size rule given)
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.epassport.gov.bd/instructions/instructions>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/bd_epassport>

## Bangladesh visa (online MRV application on visa.gov.bd) photo

- **Photo size:** 45 mm x 35 mm (35 mm wide, 45 mm high)
- **File:** JPEG; up to 300 KB
- **Background:** white (the printed visa form: 'Photograph has to be white background')
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-16
- **Official source:** <https://visa.gov.bd/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/bd_visa>
