# Taiwan: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 3 Taiwan documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-16. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Taiwan (Republic of China) passport photo (35 x 45 mm print, and a JPG of at least 413 x 531 px for the online system)

- **Photo size:** 45 mm high x 35 mm wide (1.77 x 1.38 in); 413 x 531 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 5000 KB
- **Background:** plain white, no shadows or reflections
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
- **Official source:** <https://www.boca.gov.tw/cp-140-467-29b1d-2.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/tw_passport>

## Taiwan (Republic of China) visa application photo (paper photos for a visitor or resident visa at a Taiwan overseas mission)

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** plain white, uniform lighting, no shadows or reflections
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.boca.gov.tw/cp-158-250-a5a40-2.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/tw_visa>

## Taiwan Alien Resident Certificate (ARC) photo (National Immigration Agency application form, 4.5 x 3.5 cm)

- **Photo size:** 4.5 cm high x 3.5 cm wide
- **Background:** white
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-16
- **Official source:** <https://www.immigration.gov.tw/media/109406/%E5%A4%96%E5%9C%8B%E4%BA%BA%E5%B1%85-%E5%81%9C-%E7%95%99%E6%A1%88%E4%BB%B6%E7%94%B3%E8%AB%8B%E8%A1%A8_20220801%E7%89%88.pdf>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/tw_arc>
