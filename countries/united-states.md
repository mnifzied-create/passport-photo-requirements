# United States: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 21 United States documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-18. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## ACT test registration photo (ACT Education Corp., uploaded in MyACT; prints on the admission ticket)

- **Photo size:** 480 x 640 px
- **File:** JPEG, PNG; up to 5000 KB
- **Background:** plain background (busy backgrounds might make the photo unacceptable)
