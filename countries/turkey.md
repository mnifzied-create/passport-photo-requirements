# Turkey: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 3 Turkey documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-12. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Turkish passport and identity card (kimlik kartı) biometric photo, 50 x 60 mm print

- **Photo size:** 50 mm wide x 60 mm high (not 35 x 45 mm)
- **Background:** white, without pattern and without shadows
- **Glasses:** only if you have to wear them
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-12
- **Official source:** <https://www.nvi.gov.tr/istanbul/biyometrik-fotograf-nasil-olmalidir>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/tr_passport>

## Türkiye e-Visa (evisa.gov.tr) - no photo required

- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.evisa.gov.tr/faq>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/tr_evisa>

## Türkiye residence permit (ikamet izni) biometric photo: e-İkamet upload and prints for the appointment

- **Photo size:** 5 x 6 cm (50 x 60 mm), as provincial offices state; no central size is published
- **Background:** white (e-İkamet document lists and goc.gov.tr); plain, no pattern and no shadow; the 2020 ICAO note prefers mid grey behind light hair
- **Glasses:** only if you have to wear them
- **Taken by:** someone else, not a selfie
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr/Ikamet/BasvuruIstenenBelgeler/BasvuruFormuIstenenBelgeler?tur=0>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/tr_residence_permit>
