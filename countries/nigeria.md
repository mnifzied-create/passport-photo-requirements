## Nigerian standard e-passport, online application photo upload (NIS portal, in Nigeria or abroad)

- **File:** up to 1999 KB

- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://passportintl.immigration.gov.ng/image-compliance>
