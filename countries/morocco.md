## Morocco eVisa (acces-maroc.ma) identity photo upload

- **Photo size:** 35 x 45 mm
- **File:** JPEG; up to 1000 KB
- **Background:** not stated for the eVisa beyond the ICAO standard; Moroccan consulates ask for a white background on visa photos, so white is used
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.acces-maroc.ma/assets/docs/Conditions%20utilisation%20eVisa%20-%20Fr.pdf>
