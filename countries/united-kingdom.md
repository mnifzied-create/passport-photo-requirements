# United Kingdom: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 10 United Kingdom documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-18. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Blue Badge (disabled person's parking badge), England: badge holder's photo for the GOV.UK online application or a council paper form

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high (passport size)
- **File:** PNG, GIF, JPEG; up to 20000 KB
- **Background:** light grey or cream (the regulations); the online service asks for a plain, light background with no objects behind you, a light grey or cream wall working best; strong definition between face and background
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Taken by:** someone else, not a selfie
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.gov.uk/apply-blue-badge>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/uk_blue_badge>

## Great Britain photocard driving licence (DVLA), printed passport type photo for D1 postal applications

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** plain cream or light grey, in clear contrast to the face; no shadows behind you (GOV.UK passport photo rules, linked from DVLA's pages)
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.gov.uk/renew-driving-licence>

## Northern Ireland photocard driving licence (DVA), printed photo for postal applications

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** light grey or cream for printed photos (digital photos: plain light-coloured, no patterns or textures, no objects behind you); no shadows
- **Glasses:** only if you have to wear them
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-05
- **Official source:** <https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/photo-your-driving-licence>
