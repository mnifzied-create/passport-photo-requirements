# South Africa: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 2 South Africa documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-19. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## South African passport (tourist, child, official), printed photos where not captured live

- **Photo size:** 35 mm x 45 mm
- **Background:** light grey or plain cream-coloured (DHA leaflet); white at the Tokyo mission
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.dha.gov.za/index.php/civic-services/travel-documents>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/za_passport>

## South African visitor visa and temporary residence visa, printed photos for an application at a South African embassy

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** white (South African Embassy Cotonou, quoting the Home Affairs requirements)
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://dirco.gov.za/benin/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2025/06/Requirements-for-Visitors-visa-Holiday-purposes.pdf>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/za_visa>
