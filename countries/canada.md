## Canada Temporary Resident (visitor) Visa application photo (IRCC)

- **Photo size:** at least 35 mm x 45 mm
- **Background:** plain white or light-coloured
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-16
- **Official source:** <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/temporary-resident-visa-application-photograph-specifications.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/ca_visitor_visa>

## Canada firearms licence photo: Possession and Acquisition Licence (PAL), RCMP Canadian Firearms Program

- **Photo size:** Paper applications: 45 mm wide x 57 mm high (1 3/4 x 2 1/4 inches); online: a JPG file, no size or shape set
- **File:** JPEG; 200–6000 KB
- **Background:** plain white or light-coloured, with a clear difference between the face and the background (paper form: plain light-coloured, without textures or patterns); no shadows in the background
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://rcmp.ca/en/firearms/licensing/photo-requirements-firearms-licence>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/ca_firearms_licence>

## Canada permanent residence / permanent resident (PR) card photo

- **Photo size:** 50 mm x 70 mm (2 x 2 3/4 inches); 715–2000 x 1000–2800 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 4000 KB
- **Background:** plain, untextured and white
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Taken by:** a commercial photographer or studio
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
- **Official source:** <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/permanent-residents/card/photos.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/ca_pr_card>

## Ontario security guard or private investigator licence (individuals), Private Security and Investigative Services: licence photo
