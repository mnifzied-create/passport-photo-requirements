# Botswana: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 1 Botswana document, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-16. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Botswana e-Visa (evisa.gov.bw) applicant photo

- **Photo size:** 28 mm x 36 mm (digital upload)
- **File:** JPEG; up to 30 KB
- **Background:** white (the application's photo step says white; the Photo Requirements page says plain light)
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-16
- **Official source:** <https://evisa.gov.bw/#/req_photo>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/bw_evisa>
