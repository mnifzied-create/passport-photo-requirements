# France: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 3 France documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-18. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## French driving licence (permis de conduire) photo: e-photo code (photo-signature numérique) online, or a printed photo on the photo-signature form

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** plain, light colour (light blue or light grey for example); white is forbidden
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Taken by:** a commercial photographer or studio
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F20109>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/fr_driving_licence>

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** plain, light colour (light blue or light grey for example); white is forbidden
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Taken by:** a commercial photographer or studio
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F10619>

## French residence permit (titre de séjour, carte de séjour) photo: e-photo code online, or three printed photos

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** plain, light colour (light blue or light grey for example); white is forbidden
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Taken by:** a commercial photographer or studio
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
