# Sweden: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 3 Sweden documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-19. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Swedish driving licence (körkort) renewal, printed photo sent with the application form (grundhandling) to Transportstyrelsen

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high, cleanly cut, round or sharp corners
- **Background:** light and without shadows
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/korkort/har-korkort/fornya-korkortet/fotot-pa-korkortet/>
