# Thailand: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

## Thailand e-Visa (thaievisa.go.th) applicant photograph

- **Photo size:** passport size, 3.5 x 4.5 cm shape (Dhaka mission; US consulates ask 2 x 2 in)
- **File:** JPEG; up to 3000 KB
- **Background:** white (Doha: white only; Singapore: plain white or off-white; Los Angeles: plain/blank/white)
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.thaievisa.go.th/visa/tourist-visa>
