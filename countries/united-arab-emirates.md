# United Arab Emirates: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 1 United Arab Emirates document, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-15. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## UAE Emirates ID card photo (ICP personal photo, 4.5 x 3.5 cm, white background)

- **Photo size:** 4.5 cm x 3.5 cm
- **Background:** white
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
- **Official source:** <https://icp.gov.ae/wp-content/themes/ica_v3/assets/images/servicesGuideEN2024.pdf>
