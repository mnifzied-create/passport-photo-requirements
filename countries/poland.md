# Poland: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 2 Poland documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-19. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Polish passport and ID card (dowód osobisty) photo: 35 x 45 mm print, or file for an online application

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high; 768 x 1004 px
- **File:** up to 2500 KB
- **Background:** white, evenly lit, no shadows or decorative elements (the passport act says a uniform light background)
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.gov.pl/web/gov/zdjecie-do-dowodu-lub-paszportu>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/pl_passport>

## Polish residence card (karta pobytu) photo: JPG file of at least 684 x 883 px for the MOS online application, 35 x 45 mm print

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high; 684 x 883 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 2500 KB
- **Background:** uniform light (jednolite jasne tło)
- **Glasses:** allowed, eyes clearly visible
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/553/text.pdf>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/pl_residence_card>
