# Netherlands: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 4 Netherlands documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-18. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Dutch MVV long-stay visa (provisional residence permit), printed passport photo for the embassy appointment

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** light grey, light blue or white; plain, one colour, no fade, enough contrast with the head
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
- **Official source:** <https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/mvv-long-stay/apply-ireland>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/nl_mvv_visa>

## Dutch driving licence (rijbewijs), printed colour photo for an application at the municipality (gemeente)

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** grey (Rijksoverheid page: light grey), light blue or white; plain, one colour, no gradient or shadow, enough contrast between head and background
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.rdw.nl/het-rijbewijs/aanvragen-of-verlengen>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/nl_driving_licence>

## Dutch passport and identity card (ID-kaart), printed photo

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** light grey, light blue or white; plain, one colour, no gradient, no shadow, enough contrast with the head
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/migratie-en-reizen/paspoort-en-identiteitskaart/eisen-pasfoto-paspoort-id-kaart>
