# Germany: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 5 Germany documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-18. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## German driving licence (Führerschein) photo: printed biometric photo for a Fahrerlaubnis application (Berlin LABO)

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** plain and light in one colour (ideally neutral grey), contrasting with the face and hair; light grey for dark hair, mid grey for light hair; no pattern, no shadows
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/121627/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/de_driving_licence>

## German national visa (type D: work, study, training, family reunion), printed biometric photo for the mission appointment

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** one plain bright colour, ideally neutral grey (medium grey behind light hair, light grey behind dark hair), contrasting with face and hair; no pattern, no shadows
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
- **Official source:** <https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/2775146-2775146>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/de_national_visa>

## German passport (Reisepass) and identity card photo, applied for at a German embassy or consulate abroad (printed 35x45 mm)

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** plain single colour, shadow-free, in clear contrast with face and hair; preferably light grey (BMI template); Addis Ababa: white or light grey
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://wien.diplo.de/at-de/service/2447102-2447102>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/de_passport_abroad>
