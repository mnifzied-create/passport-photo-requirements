# Denmark: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 2 Denmark documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-19. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Danish driving licence (kørekort), printed photo you bring to Borgerservice for a new licence or a renewal

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** light, without shadows or patterns (borger.dk); a plain, even light colour, for example light blue or light grey, no shadows; sharp contrast for white or blond hair or a light headscarf (Færdselsstyrelsen sheet)
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://www.borger.dk/transport-trafik-rejser/Biler-og-koerekort/koerekort/forny-dit-koerekort>
