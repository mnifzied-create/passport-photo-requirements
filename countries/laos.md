# Laos: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 1 Laos document, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-05. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Laos eVisa (laoevisa.gov.la) applicant photo

- **Photo size:** 4 x 6 cm (digital upload); 300 x 300 px
- **File:** JPEG, PNG; up to 10000 KB
- **Background:** plain white, no shadows
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-05
- **Official source:** <https://laoevisa.gov.la/info>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/la_evisa>
