# Ireland: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 3 Ireland documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-15. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Irish passport book or passport card, online application (digital photo)

- **Photo size:** 715 x 951 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 9000 KB
- **Background:** completely plain light grey, white or cream, with enough contrast
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Taken by:** someone else, not a selfie
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.ireland.ie/en/dfa/passports/photo-guidelines/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/ie_passport_online>

## Irish passport for a baby or young child, online application (digital photo)

- **Photo size:** 715 x 951 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 9000 KB
- **Background:** completely plain light grey, white or cream with enough contrast; a baby who cannot support themselves lies on a plain, white surface
- **Taken by:** someone else, not a selfie
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.ireland.ie/en/dfa/passports/photo-guidelines/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/ie_passport_baby>

## Irish visa application photos: two identical printed photos, 35 to 38 mm wide and 45 to 50 mm high

- **Photo size:** 35 to 38 mm wide x 45 to 50 mm high (35 x 45 mm is the smallest allowed)
- **Background:** plain white or light grey, with good contrast between your face and the background; no shadows or glare
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
- **Official source:** <https://www.irishimmigration.ie/photograph-rules-for-visa-applications/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/ie_visa>
