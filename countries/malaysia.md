# Malaysia: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 2 Malaysia documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-15. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Malaysia Employment Pass and expatriate pass photo (ESD Online, 35 x 50 mm, at most 25 KB)

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 50 mm high
- **File:** up to 25 KB
- **Background:** plain white, no border or frame (required for every pass type from 1 December 2026; until then plain blue is also accepted for VDR applications)
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
- **Official source:** <https://esd.imi.gov.my/portal/photo-requirements/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/my_employment_pass>

## Malaysia eVISA photo (MyVISA portal; Visa Without Reference rules, Visa With Reference noted)

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 50 mm high
- **File:** up to 2000 KB
- **Background:** plain white, no border or frame (VTR); plain white or plain blue (VDR)
- **Taken by:** a commercial photographer or studio
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/assets/faq/howto-photo-upload.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/my_evisa>
