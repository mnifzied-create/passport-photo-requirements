# Ghana: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 2 Ghana documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-19. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Ghana biometric passport, online application photo upload (passport.mfa.gov.gh, in Ghana or at a mission)

- **Photo size:** 140 x 170 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 250 KB
- **Background:** white
- **Glasses:** not allowed
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://passport.mfa.gov.gh/faqs>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/gh_passport>

## Ghana eVisa (evisa.immigration.gov.gh) applicant photo: tourist (B2) and business (B1) visas

- **Photo size:** about 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm (digital upload)
- **Background:** plain white or light; not busy, dark, textured or distracting
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://evisa.immigration.gov.gh/applicant-guide.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/gh_visa>
