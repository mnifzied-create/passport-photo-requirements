## Kuwait e-visa face photo (Ministry of Interior Kuwait Visa platform: tourist and family visit visas)

- **Photo size:** 30 mm wide x 40 mm high (digital upload); 720 x 720 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 2000 KB
- **Background:** white (FAQ); plain white or light grey, plain, even and uniform, no shadows or glare (upload instructions)
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://kuwaitvisa.moi.gov.kw/assets/i18n/en.json>
