# South Korea: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 7 South Korea documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-19. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## EPS-TOPIK (Korean Language Test for the Employment Permit System, 고용허가제 한국어능력시험) application photo, printed and attached to the application sheet (HRD Korea)

- **Photo size:** 3.5 cm wide x 4.5 cm high
- **Background:** not stated by HRD Korea
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://epstopik.hrdkorea.or.kr/epstopik/recp/guide/selectVisitGuideDesc.do?lang=en>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/kr_eps_topik>

- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/board/viewboardlist.do?tmpltNm=faq2&category=&keyWord=&pageIndex=4&locale=EN>

## South Korea Residence Card / Alien Registration Card (외국인등록증) photo, 3.5 x 4.5 cm (Ministry of Justice standard on HiKorea)

- **Photo size:** 3.5 cm wide x 4.5 cm high
- **Background:** white background, no background scenery, no border (흰색바탕, 무배경으로 테두리가 없어야 합니다)
- **Glasses:** allowed, eyes clearly visible
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.hikorea.go.kr/info/InfoDatail.pt?CAT_SEQ=182&PARENT_ID=139>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/kr_residence_card>

## South Korea driver's licence photo (운전면허증 사진), 3.5 x 4.5 cm print or 413 x 531 px JPG online (Korea Road Traffic Authority standard)

- **Photo size:** 3.5 cm wide x 4.5 cm high; 413 x 531 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 500 KB
- **Background:** white background (흰색 배경), no background scenery (무배경); proper lighting that keeps your natural skin colour
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.safedriving.or.kr/etGuide/selectEtGuide04.do>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/kr_driving_licence>

## South Korea visa application photo (visa form and Korea Visa Portal e-Form, 3.5 x 4.5 cm)

- **Photo size:** 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm (US posts: 2 x 2 in)
- **Background:** white (Korea Visa Portal); the visa form and some embassies also accept off-white
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.visa.go.kr/openPage.do?MENU_ID=10204&LANG_TYPE=EN>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/kr_visa>

## South Korean passport photo (3.5 x 4.5 cm print, or the online reissue file of about 413 x 531 px)

- **Photo size:** 3.5 cm wide x 4.5 cm high; 395–431 x 507–550 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 500 KB
- **Background:** uniform white, no ink marks, no border
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-12
- **Official source:** <https://www.passport.go.kr/home/kor/contents.do?menuPos=32>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/kr_passport>

## TOPIK (Test of Proficiency in Korean, 한국어능력시험) application photo, uploaded at registration on topik.go.kr (NIIED)

- **Photo size:** 3.5 cm wide x 4.5 cm high (digital upload: 413 x 532 pixels); 413 x 532 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 200 KB
- **Background:** white background (흰색 바탕); Ministry of Justice standard: white background and frameless
- **Glasses:** allowed, eyes clearly visible
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.topik.go.kr/TWGUID/TWGUID0070.do>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/kr_topik>
