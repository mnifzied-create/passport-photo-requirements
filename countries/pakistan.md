# Pakistan: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 2 Pakistan documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-05. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Pakistan online visa (Pakistan Online Visa System, visa.nadra.gov.pk) applicant photograph

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high; 350 x 467 px
- **File:** JPEG, PNG; up to 350 KB
- **Background:** plain white (photograph guide and application guide); the guide's style section also says a plain light-coloured background
- **Glasses:** not allowed
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-05
- **Official source:** <https://visa.nadra.gov.pk/photograph-guide/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/pk_evisa>

## Pakistani passport, online application for Pakistanis abroad (DGI&P e-Services portal, photo upload)

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high (as a digital file of at most 20KB)
- **File:** JPEG, PNG; up to 20 KB
- **Background:** plain white
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Taken by:** a commercial photographer or studio
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk/photo-requirements>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/pk_passport>
