# Greece: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 1 Greece document, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-04. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Greek passport photo (digital via myPhoto in Greece; upload or print at consulates abroad)

- **Photo size:** 1280 x 1600 px
- **File:** JPEG
- **Background:** digital file: grey, about RGB 190,190,190 (+/- 10); printed photo abroad (2018 sheet): white, cream or 5% grey
- **Glasses:** only if you have to wear them
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://passport.gov.gr/inner.php/diadikasia-ekdosis/documents/downloads/texnikes-prodiagrafes-photo/specificationphoto.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/gr_passport>
