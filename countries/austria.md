# Austria: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 4 Austria documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-18. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Austrian driving licence (Scheckkartenführerschein), printed photo 35 x 45 mm for the Führerscheinbehörde or driving school

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high (Hochformat)
- **Background:** one plain colour, light, ideally grey (mid grey behind light hair, light grey behind dark hair); no pattern; enough contrast with face and hair
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/persoenliche_dokumente_und_bestaetigungen/fuehrerschein/1/Seite.040170>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/at_driving_licence>

## Austrian passport (Reisepass) and identity card (Personalausweis), printed photo

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high

- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.bmi.gv.at/607/passbild_kriterien.html>
