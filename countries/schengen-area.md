## Schengen short-stay visa (type C) application photo, ICAO format

- **Photo size:** 35 mm x 45 mm (France-Visas: 35–40 mm wide)
- **Background:** plain light-coloured (France-Visas); white (German mission in Dhaka)
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://france-visas.gouv.fr/en/faq>
