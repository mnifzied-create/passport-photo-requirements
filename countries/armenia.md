# Armenia: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 1 Armenia document, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-05. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Armenia e-Visa (evisa.mfa.am) facial photo upload

- **Photo size:** 250–6000 x 250–6000 px
- **File:** JPEG, PNG; up to 6000 KB
- **Background:** not coloured: the photo requirements panel lists 'Back side is colored' among Not Valid Photos; its valid examples are on plain white to light grey
- **Glasses:** not allowed
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-05
- **Official source:** <https://evisa.mfa.am/i18n/en.json>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/am_evisa>
