The photo rules of 7 Singapore documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-18. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

- **File:** JPEG, PNG, HEIC, HEIF; up to 8000 KB

- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15

## Singapore entry visa application photo (ICA e-Service upload 400 x 514 px, or a 35 x 45 mm print with Form 14A)

- **Background:** plain white, no shadows
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
