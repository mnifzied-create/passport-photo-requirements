# Hong Kong: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 2 Hong Kong documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-19. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Hong Kong International Driving Permit (Transport Department, form TD51): printed photos for a paper application

- **Photo size:** 40 mm wide x 50 mm high
- **Background:** plain background, no decoration
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://www.td.gov.hk/filemanager/common/td51_e-fillable_eng.pdf>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/hk_international_driving_permit>

## Hong Kong SAR passport photo (40 x 50 mm print, or a JPEG of at least 1200 x 1600 px for the online application)

- **Photo size:** 40 mm wide x 50 mm high; 1200 x 1600 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 5000 KB
- **Background:** plain white
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
- **Official source:** <https://www.immd.gov.hk/eng/residents/immigration/traveldoc/photorequirements.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/hk_passport>
