# Qatar: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 1 Qatar document, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-04. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Qatar e-visa personal photo (Hayya platform: tourist and other Hayya entry visas)

- **Photo size:** 30 mm wide x 40 mm high (digital upload); 720 x 720 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 2000 KB
- **Background:** plain white or light grey, even and uniform, no shadows or glare
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://beta-cms.hayya.qa/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Hayya-Guidelines-EN-2.pdf>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/qa_visa>
