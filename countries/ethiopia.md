# Ethiopia: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 1 Ethiopia document, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-05. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Ethiopia e-Visa (evisa.gov.et) applicant photo

- **Photo size:** 2 x 2 inches
- **File:** JPEG, PNG; up to 2000 KB
- **Background:** white (photo tips); a blurred or unclean background is not acceptable (upload notes)
- **Taken by:** someone else, not a selfie
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-05
- **Official source:** <https://www.evisa.gov.et/static/js/main.ccc32951.js>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/et_evisa>
