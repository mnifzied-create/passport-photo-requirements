# New Zealand: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 6 New Zealand documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-18. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Kiwi Access Card (formerly the 18+ Card), Hospitality New Zealand: printed photos for the paper application

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high (portrait)
- **Background:** plain and light, not white, with no shadows, objects or other people
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Taken by:** someone else, not a selfie
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://kiwiaccess.co.nz/photo-requirements/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/nz_kiwi_access_card>

## New Zealand Electronic Travel Authority (NZeTA) photo, online form

- **Photo size:** 900–2250 x 1200–3000 px
- **File:** JPEG; 512–3000 KB
- **Background:** plain and neutral, good contrast with the face; INZ photographer guide: plain, light-coloured (not white)
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Taken by:** someone else, not a selfie
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.immigration.govt.nz/process-to-apply/applying-for-a-visa/applying-online/uploading-documents-and-photos/visa-and-nzeta-photos/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/nz_nzeta>

## New Zealand firearms licence photo (Firearms Safety and Education New Zealand, MyFirearms online application)

- **Photo size:** Digital: JPG or JPEG, portrait 4:3, 900-4500 px wide x 1200-6000 px high (paper form example: 35 mm wide x 45 mm high); 900–4500 x 1200–6000 px
- **File:** JPEG; 25–10000 KB
- **Background:** plain, light-coloured
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/individual-licence/get-licensed/you-apply>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/nz_firearms_licence>

## New Zealand passport (digital photo)

- **Photo size:** 900–4500 x 1200–6000 px
- **File:** JPEG; 250–5000 KB
- **Background:** plain, light-coloured, strong contrast with the face, no shadows or objects
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Taken by:** someone else, not a selfie
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.passports.govt.nz/passport-photos>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/nz_passport>

## New Zealand passport for a baby or child (digital photo)

- **Photo size:** 900–4500 x 1200–6000 px
- **File:** JPEG; 250–5000 KB
- **Background:** a plain, light-coloured sheet under the baby, photographed from above; strong contrast with the face, no shadows or objects
- **Taken by:** someone else, not a selfie
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.passports.govt.nz/passport-photos>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/nz_passport_baby>
