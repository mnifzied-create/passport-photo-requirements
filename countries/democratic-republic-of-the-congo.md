# Democratic Republic of the Congo: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 1 Democratic Republic of the Congo document, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-16. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## DR Congo eVisa (evisa.gouv.cd) ID photo upload

- **Photo size:** 100 x 200 px
- **File:** JPEG, PNG; up to 3000 KB
- **Background:** plain and light (required from 1 December 2026; no background rule is stated before that date)
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-16
- **Official source:** <https://evisa.gouv.cd/build/assets/appearance-C17ERn_j.js>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/cd_evisa>
