# Vietnam: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 3 Vietnam documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-19. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Vietnam e-Visa (evisa.gov.vn) applicant portrait photo

- **Photo size:** 4 x 6 cm (digital upload)
- **File:** JPEG; up to 2000 KB
- **Background:** white
- **Glasses:** not allowed
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://evisa.gov.vn/instruction>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/vn_evisa>

## Vietnam ordinary passport photo (hộ chiếu phổ thông, 4 x 6 cm, white background)

- **Photo size:** 4 cm x 6 cm
- **Background:** white
- **Glasses:** not allowed
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
- **Official source:** <https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/thu-tuc-cap-ho-chieu-pho-thong-o-trong-nuoc-tai-cong-an-cap-tinh-119260714162349301.htm>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/vn_passport>

## Vietnam temporary residence card for foreigners (thẻ tạm trú), printed photo 2 x 3 cm for the NA8 application form

- **Photo size:** 2 cm wide x 3 cm high
- **Background:** white (phông nền màu trắng)
- **Glasses:** allowed, eyes clearly visible
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://congan.hungyen.gov.vn/updload_ubt/userfiles/files/N%E1%BB%99i%20dung%2008%20TTHC%20CATHY.pdf>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/vn_temporary_residence_card>
