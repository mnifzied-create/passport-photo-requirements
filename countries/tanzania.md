# Tanzania: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 1 Tanzania document, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-05. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Tanzania e-Visa (visa.immigration.go.tz) applicant photo

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high (digital upload)
- **File:** JPEG; up to 500 KB
- **Background:** plain (no colour named); no distracting shadows or images on the background
- **Glasses:** allowed, eyes clearly visible
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-05
- **Official source:** <https://visa.immigration.go.tz/guidelines>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/tz_evisa>
