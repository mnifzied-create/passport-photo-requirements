# Russia: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 3 Russia documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-19. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Russian internal passport (паспорт гражданина Российской Федерации), printed personal photos for issue or replacement

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** white, even, with no stripes, spots, other objects or shadows
- **Glasses:** allowed, eyes clearly visible
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/file/pdf?eoNumber=0001202012310102>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/ru_internal_passport>

## Russian international passport (заграничный паспорт), 5-year passport without a chip: printed photos for applications to the Russian Foreign Ministry and its consulates

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** white, even, with no stripes, spots, other objects or shadows
- **Glasses:** allowed, eyes clearly visible
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://www.kdmid.ru/cons/passports/list-of-documents-required-for-registration-of-foreign-passports/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/ru_passport>

## Russian unified e-visa (единая электронная виза) applicant face photo

- **File:** JPEG
- **Background:** plain light-coloured, no shadows
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://evisa.kdmid.ru/Home/Instruction>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/ru_evisa>
