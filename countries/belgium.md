## Belgian passport and electronic identity card (eID), printed photo

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** light-coloured, plain and even, no colour gradient, enough contrast with the head; strong blue or red backgrounds excluded
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://diplomatie.belgium.be/en/belgians-abroad/belgian-passport/ordinary-passport>
