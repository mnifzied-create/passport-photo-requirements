## Australian citizenship application photo (ImmiAccount upload; 35-40 x 45-50 mm print for paper and Form 1195)

- **Photo size:** 35-40 mm wide x 45-50 mm high (35 x 45 mm is the smallest allowed)
- **File:** JPEG; 70–3500 KB
- **Background:** neutral or light grey, contrasting with the face; no shadows on the background
- **Glasses:** not allowed
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/citizenship/photo-requirements-for-citizenship-applications>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/au_citizenship>

## Australian passport (printed photos)

- **Photo size:** 35–40 mm wide x 45–50 mm high (35 x 45 mm is the smallest allowed)
- **Background:** plain white or light grey, contrasting with the face
- **Glasses:** not allowed
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.passports.gov.au/help/passport-photos>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/au_passport>
