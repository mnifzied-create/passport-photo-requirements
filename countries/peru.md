## Peruvian visa (tourist and similar) application photo, as set by each Peruvian consulate

- **Photo size:** passport size; 3.5 x 4.5 cm, 35 x 43 mm or 2 x 2 in depending on the consulate
- **Background:** white (Santiago: plain white)
- **Glasses:** not allowed
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
