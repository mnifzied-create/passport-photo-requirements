# Jamaica: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 1 Jamaica document, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-04. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Jamaican passport photo (35 x 45 mm prints; digital photo for the PICA online adult renewal)

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **File:** JPEG
- **Background:** plain, no shadows: white for a dark complexion, pastel shaded for a light complexion, pale blue or pastel for grey/white hair (simplified renewal sheet: off-white, pale blue for grey/white or bleached hair)
- **Glasses:** only if you have to wear them
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.pica.gov.jm/passport/photo-requirements>
