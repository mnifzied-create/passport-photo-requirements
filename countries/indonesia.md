# Indonesia: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 2 Indonesia documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-12. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Indonesia e-Visa and e-VOA (evisa.imigrasi.go.id) profile photo

- **Photo size:** 4 x 6 cm (digital upload, at least 400 x 600 pixels); 400 x 600 px
- **File:** JPEG, PNG; up to 2000 KB
- **Background:** not stated on the e-Visa portal; Indonesian missions ask for a white background (Caracas, embassy-issued visas)
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/front/register/wna>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/id_evisa>
