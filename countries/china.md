# China: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 4 China documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-19. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## China residence permit for foreigners (外国人居留许可 / 外国人居留证件) photo: 33x48 mm print and 354-480 x 472-640 px digital

- **Photo size:** 33 mm wide x 48 mm high; 354–480 x 472–640 px
- **File:** JPEG; 20–80 KB
- **Background:** uniform white, light grey or light blue, even, no gradient, no shadows, no other people or objects (Beijing: white; Shanwei: white or light blue)
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741445/n763221/index.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/cn_residence_permit>

## Chinese passport and travel document (旅行证) photo for citizens abroad (China Consular app upload; 33x48 mm prints)

- **Photo size:** 33 mm wide x 48 mm high
- **File:** JPEG, PNG; up to 4999 KB
- **Background:** pure white, even, no shadows (MFA form: uniform white)
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://cs.mfa.gov.cn/zggmcgx/hzlxz_149470/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/cn_passport>

## Chinese visa application photo (paper 33x48 mm and online-form digital photo)

- **Photo size:** 33 mm wide x 48 mm high; 354–420 x 472–560 px
- **File:** JPEG; 40–120 KB
- **Background:** white (some posts: white or close to white), no border
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://cr.china-embassy.gov.cn/esp/lsyw/aviso/202312/t20231220_11207351.htm>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/cn_visa>

## Mainland Travel Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Residents (港澳居民來往內地通行證, Home Return Permit / 回鄉證) photo, applications in Hong Kong at China Travel Service: 33x48 mm print and 354x472 px digital

- **Photo size:** 33 mm wide x 48 mm high; 354 x 472 px
- **File:** JPEG; 20–80 KB
- **Background:** pure white, no gradient, no shadows, no other people or objects (Home Return Permit and one-time Exit-Entry Permit)
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://www.ctshk.com/mep/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/photoStandard.pdf>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/cn_mainland_travel_permit>
