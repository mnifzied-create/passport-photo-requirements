# Czech Republic: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 2 Czech Republic documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-15. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Czech residence permit photo (Ministry of the Interior), printed 35 x 45 mm

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** white to light blue or light grey; a smooth transition between these colours is allowed
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
- **Official source:** <https://ipc.gov.cz/en/forms-and-documents/documents/photograph/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/cz_residence_permit>

## Czech visa application photo (Schengen and long-term visa), printed 35 x 45 mm

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
- **Background:** white to light blue or light grey (Interior Ministry); white or light (Embassy in Tokyo)
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
- **Official source:** <https://mzv.gov.cz/jnp/en/information_for_aliens/supporting_documents_overview/photo.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/cz_visa>
