# Spain: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 3 Spain documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-18. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Spanish driving licence (permiso de conducir, DGT), printed photo 32 x 26 mm

- **Photo size:** 26 mm wide x 32 mm high
- **Background:** plain (fondo liso); no colour is stated
- **Glasses:** allowed, eyes clearly visible
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://sede.dgt.gob.es/es/permisos-de-conducir/canjes-de-permisos/canjes-de-permisos-extranjeros/canjes-inscripcion-renovacion-y-sustitucion-de-permisos-de-la-ue-y-eee/canje-de-permisos-de-la-ue-y-eee/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/es_driving_licence>

## Spanish foreigner identity card (TIE, Tarjeta de Identidad de Extranjero), printed photo 32 x 26 mm

- **Photo size:** 26 mm wide x 32 mm high (written '32 por 26 milímetros')
- **Background:** plain, uniform white ('fondo uniforme blanco y liso')
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.policia.es/_es/extranjeria_extranjeros.php>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/es_tie>

## Spanish passport and DNI (national identity card), printed photo 32 x 26 mm

- **Photo size:** 26 mm wide x 32 mm high (written '32 x 26 mm')
- **Background:** plain, uniform white
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2003-13978>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/es_passport>
