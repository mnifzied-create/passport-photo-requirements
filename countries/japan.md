# Japan: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 7 Japan documents, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-19. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## JAPAN eVISA (evisa.mofa.go.jp) facial photo upload

- **File:** JPEG, PNG, GIF, HEIC; up to 2000 KB
- **Background:** plain and untextured (no colour named)
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-16
- **Official source:** <https://www.evisa.mofa.go.jp/index>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/jp_evisa>

## JLPT (Japanese-Language Proficiency Test, 日本語能力試験) application face photo, uploaded on MyJLPT when applying to sit the test in Japan (JEES)

- **Photo size:** 480 x 640 px
- **File:** JPEG; up to 5000 KB
- **Background:** not stated by JEES
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-19
- **Official source:** <https://info.jees-jlpt.jp/myjlpt/application.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/jp_jlpt>

## Japan My Number Card (マイナンバーカード) application photo (J-LIS: 4.5 x 3.5 cm print by post, or a JPEG file online)

- **Photo size:** 4.5 cm high x 3.5 cm wide (postal application); 480–6000 x 480–6000 px
- **File:** JPEG; 20–7000 KB
- **Background:** no background (無背景): no chair or objects behind you, no shadow, no pattern
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.kojinbango-card.go.jp/apprec/apply/facephoto/>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/jp_my_number_card>

## Japan driver's licence application photo (運転免許 申請用写真 / 持参写真), 3.0 cm high x 2.4 cm wide (Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department standard)

- **Photo size:** 2.4 cm wide x 3.0 cm high
- **Background:** no background (無背景): no scenery, no patterned wallpaper, curtains or wood grain, and the face outline and clothes must not blend into the background
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-18
- **Official source:** <https://www.keishicho.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/menkyo/koshin/koshin/koshin02_2.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/jp_driving_licence>

## Japan residence card and Certificate of Eligibility application photo (Immigration Services Agency, 4 cm x 3 cm)

- **Photo size:** 4 cm high x 3 cm wide
- **File:** JPEG
- **Background:** no background, shadows included; a dark background that hides the outline is refused
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-15
- **Official source:** <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/status/photo_info_00002.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/jp_residence_card>

## Japan visa application photo (paper form at an embassy, consulate or accredited agency)

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high (MOFA form: 45mm x 35mm or 2in x 1.4in; some posts differ)
- **Background:** plain; most posts say white (Mumbai, Denver, Malaysia: white; Chicago: white or off-white; Houston: white or blue; Azerbaijan: plain colour-neutral, not necessarily white)
- **Glasses:** not allowed
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://www.mofa.go.jp/files/000124525.pdf>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/jp_visa>

## Japanese passport (旅券) application photo, printed 35 x 45 mm or uploaded in the online application (Mynaportal / ORRnet)

- **Photo size:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high, no border
- **File:** JPEG; 20–600 KB
- **Background:** no background (plain, shadows included); white recommended, the traditional pale blue also accepted; no gradation, no pattern or textured wallcovering, no objects, not dark; clear contrast with face and hair
- **Glasses:** allowed with no tint and no glare
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-05
- **Official source:** <https://www.mofa.go.jp/mofaj/toko/passport/ic_photo.html>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/jp_passport>
