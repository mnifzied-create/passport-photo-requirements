# Myanmar: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

The photo rules of 1 Myanmar document, each read on the issuing authority's own page. Last read 2026-11-05. Part of [Passport, visa and ID photo requirements](../README.md), by [WinPhoto ID](https://winphoto.io/id).

## Myanmar eVisa (evisa.moip.gov.mm) applicant photo: tourist and business eVisa

- **Photo size:** 3.8 x 4.6 cm, or 1.5 x 1.8 inches (digital upload)
- **File:** JPEG; up to 2000 KB
- **Background:** plain white or off-white
- **Glasses:** not allowed
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-05
- **Official source:** <https://evisa.moip.gov.mm/assets/dist/img/photo-req.png>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/mm_evisa>
