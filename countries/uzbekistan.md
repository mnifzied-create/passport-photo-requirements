# Uzbekistan: passport, visa and ID photo requirements

## Uzbekistan e-visa (e-visa.gov.uz) face photo upload

- **Photo size:** 35 x 45 mm at 300 dpi (digital upload)
- **File:** JPEG; up to 1000 KB
- **Background:** not stated in words; the face image must follow ICAO Doc 9303, and the portal's own example of an accepted photo has a plain white background
- **Rules read:** 2026-11-04
- **Official source:** <https://e-visa.gov.uz/assets/localization/en.json>
- **Quoted rules and a free check of your own photo:** <https://winphoto.io/id/uz_evisa>
